# Big Scroll

A personalized, swipeable Wikipedia feed.

[Try it live](https://p3rciv3l.github.io/big-scroll/)

The feed learns from your likes, dislikes, "Not interested" choices, article clicks, and time spent reading. It compares words in article titles, categories, and summaries to rank new articles toward your interests, with some randomness for variety. "Not interested" also hides that article. Your feedback and preferences stay in your browser.
